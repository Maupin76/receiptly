🧾 ScanLedger — Smart Receipt Scanner & Expense Ledger

Stop stuffing paper receipts into shoeboxes and spending hours typing numbers into spreadsheets.

ScanLedger is a fast, mobile-first expense tracker built specifically for food trucks, restaurants, caterers, and small business owners. Snap a photo of any receipt—or an entire multi-page supplier invoice—and ScanLedger automatically reads every line item, sorts your spending into detailed categories, and builds ready-to-use spreadsheets for you and your bookkeeper.

🌟 Why Use ScanLedger?

📸 Scan Single Receipts or Multi-Page Invoices: Buying supplies from Restaurant Depot, Sysco, US Foods, Costco, or Sam's Club? Snap Page 1, Page 2, Page 3, and beyond in one session. ScanLedger combines the entire invoice into a single record without double-counting page subtotals.

🤖 Automatic Line-Item Sorting: Instead of lumping a $600 supply run into a single "Supplies" number, ScanLedger reads every item on the page and breaks down your true costs:

🥩 Meat & Seafood: Beef, Chicken, Pork, Shrimp, Fish/Seafood, Turkey/Lamb

🥄 Consumables & Disposables: Spoons, Forks, Knives, Takeout Containers, Napkins & Towels, Cups & Lids, Foil & Film, Gloves

🍳 Utensils & Smallwares: Tongs & Turners, Knives & Cutting Boards, Pans & Cambros, Ladles & Scoops, Thermometers & Scales

🔥 Equipment: Fryers & Baskets, Cooking Appliances, Refrigeration, Prep Tables & Shelving, Small Appliances

🥬 Produce, Dairy & Eggs, Dry Goods, Beverages, Cleaning & Custom Categories

📊 Real-Time Spending Breakdown: Instantly see how much of your budget is going to Beef vs. Chicken vs. Shrimp, or compare spending across different suppliers and dates.

📑 One-Tap Spreadsheet Export: Download clean .CSV files or copy your itemized ledger directly into Google Sheets, Microsoft Excel, or QuickBooks whenever it's time to review food costs or file taxes.

☁️ Private Cloud Sync Across Devices: Create a free account once and access your receipts, custom categories, and expense totals from your phone, tablet, or desktop computer.

📲 How to Install ScanLedger on Your Phone

ScanLedger installs directly onto your phone's Home Screen—no app store clutter or heavy downloads required:

On iPhone & iPad (Safari)

Open the ScanLedger web link in Safari.

Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom of your screen).

Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

Tap Add in the top-right corner.

On Android (Chrome)

Open the ScanLedger web link in Chrome.

Tap the Menu (⋮) icon in the top-right corner.

Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

Follow the prompt to place ScanLedger right alongside your other apps.

🚀 Getting Started in 3 Simple Steps

Create Your Free Account: Sign up with your email and password so your receipts are safely backed up to your private cloud ledger.

Snap & Verify: Tap Take Photo or Open Camera to capture one or multiple pages of a receipt. Review the extracted items, retailer name, date, tax, and total—or create your own custom categories—and tap Save Receipt to Expenses.

Track & Export: Check the Breakdown tab anytime to monitor food and supply costs, or head to the Export tab to download your spreadsheet for any date range.

🔒 Your Data & Privacy

Private by Design: Every user's receipts, supplier history, and custom categories are isolated inside their own password-protected account.

Always Accessible: Even if you upgrade or replace your phone, simply log back in with your email and password to restore your complete receipt history.
